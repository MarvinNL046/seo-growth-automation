## The structural facts (her claims, order-of-magnitude reliable)

- ~58.5% of US Google searches end without any click; per 1,000 searches,
  roughly 360 clicks reach the open web at all (2024 clickstream).
- Search volume keeps growing while click-through shrinks. Impressions up
  with clicks flat — the "alligator graph" — is the structural pattern
  showing up in GSC, not automatically a failed action.
- The first impression of a site is now the SERP snippet or an AI answer,
  not the homepage.

## What this changes in a run's judgment

1. **Do not read rising impressions with lagging clicks as a regression.**
   Check position and snippet quality first; judge CTR on the page
   dimension against position-appropriate expectations.
2. **CTR work on existing impressions outranks new head-term content.**
   This is why title/description/answer-block repairs score high in the
   action choice — the reference confirms the bias, it does not add a rule.
3. **The snippet is the homepage.** Titles, descriptions and FAQ answers
   should answer the query with the brand visible, so a zero-click
   impression still deposits recall.
4. **Branded queries are a leading indicator.** When baselines allow, note
   whether branded impressions grow; that is trust compounding even when
   clicks do not.
5. **Traffic is not the KPI.** The per-site success metric (defined in the
   site profile or PRODUCT.md, e.g. affiliate revenue) stays the goal;
   never celebrate impression growth as an outcome in a run report.
