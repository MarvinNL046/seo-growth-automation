# Financial/YMYL source policy

Use this policy for mortgage and loan content.

- Treat rates, thresholds, programme rules, tax treatment, legal obligations, repayment guidance, and affordability claims as time-sensitive.
- Browse and cite current primary sources such as official government agencies, regulators, programme administrators, or statutory publications.
- Record source URL, publisher, page title, access date, and the exact claim supported in the run report.
- Distinguish calculator estimates from offers, advice, approval, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Show important assumptions and exclusions next to the result, not only in a footer.
- Research every factual FAQ answer. A real PAA question with a plausible but unsupported answer must not ship.
- Do not update `lastReviewedAt` unless the complete affected page and its claims were genuinely re-reviewed.
- Stop when jurisdiction, effective date, or primary-source support is unclear.
